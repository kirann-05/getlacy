Read Me · SimpleText

Read Me First

Which Macs does it run on?

macOS 14 or later, on both Apple silicon and Intel. If your Mac has no notch, Lacy draws one for you.

Is it free?

Yes, for the whole beta. We'll talk about 1.0 later.

macOS says "Lacy Not Opened". Help?

The beta isn't notarized yet. Right-click Lacy in Applications, choose Open, then Open again. The disk image has an "Open Me First" note that walks you through it.

Will it slow my Mac down?

It idles at 0.0% CPU and about 31 MB of memory. Nothing polls.

Does it hide in full screen?

Over full-screen apps the notch turns invisible and lets clicks through until you point right at it. It also steps aside while you're in the app that's playing.

Can it hide from screenshots?

Yes. It's optional, and the setting also covers screen sharing.

What does it collect?

Nothing. There's no account and no analytics. Updates come from a public GitHub repository.
